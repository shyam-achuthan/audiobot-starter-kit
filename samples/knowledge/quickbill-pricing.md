# QuickBill plans and pricing

QuickBill is billing, GST invoicing and inventory software for retail shops, pharmacies and small distributors in India. It works on Android, Windows and in the browser.

## Plans

**Starter plan: ₹499 per month** (₹4,999 per year). One user, one shop, GST invoices, WhatsApp bill sharing, basic stock tracking and daily sales reports.

**Business plan: ₹1,299 per month** (₹12,999 per year). Up to 5 users and 2 shops, everything in Starter plus barcode scanning, purchase orders, supplier management, low-stock alerts and Tally export.

**Enterprise plan: custom pricing.** Unlimited users and shops, dedicated account manager, API access, custom reports and on-site training. A specialist prepares a quote.

All prices exclude 18% GST.

## Trial and discounts

Every plan has a 14-day free trial, no credit card needed. Annual billing saves about two months compared with monthly billing. Registered NGOs and educational institutions get 20% off any plan.

## Add-ons

Extra user: ₹199 per user per month. Extra shop location: ₹499 per shop per month. Thermal printer and barcode scanner bundle: ₹6,500 one time.

## Payment

We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and NEFT. Invoices are emailed on the billing date.
